For Maa ❤️

I don’t say this enough…

but thank you for everything you do.

From small things…
to the things I didn’t even notice…

you’ve always been there.

Maa,

I may not always express it properly…
but I see your efforts.

The way you take care of everything…
the way you worry…
the way you support silently…

I know I don’t say thank you enough.

But today I just want you to know —
I’m really grateful to have you.

You are my strength… even when I don’t show it.

— Your son, Aditya
